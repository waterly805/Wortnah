# Wortnah 0.2.0

## Clearer German audio

- Uses exact `de-DE` Standard German voices when the browser provides them.
- Adds Automatic, Female, and Male voice preferences.
- Adds Slow, Calm, and Normal speaking speeds.
- Adds a voice test sentence.
- Removes automatic reading of every option on screen; the first tap reads one choice and the second confirms it.

Browser and operating-system voice availability varies. When the requested male or female voice is unavailable, Wortnah uses the best available `de-DE` voice rather than selecting an Austrian or Swiss German locale.

## Adjustable choice count

- Mein Bereich can choose 2, 4, 6, or 8 visible options.
- Eight is the default for new use.
- Settings show an immediate layout preview.
- The four-purpose starting screen remains four choices because it contains exactly four communication intentions.

## Missing-option reporting

- “Etwas fehlt” is available throughout every communication step.
- Reports record the screen, current path, selected choice ID, and visible choice count.
- Reports do not store message text or audio.
- Begleitung sees the total number of missing-option reports on the dashboard.
