# Wortnah 0.3.0

## AI cost controller

- AI is disabled by default.
- Monthly AI request, input-token, output-token, and cost limits all begin at zero.
- The controller permits only a future server-side, approved AI feature to write usage records.
- Normal communication, browser speech, messages, and KPI events use no AI tokens.
- Begleitung can see AI status, requests, tokens, and estimated cost without seeing prompts, messages, recordings, or photographs.

## Usage-over-time dashboard

- Tracks significant privacy-safe activity from the first real use.
- Shows weekly messages, active days, important messages, and missing-content reports.
- Shows a compact seven-day activity trend.
- Records screen views, choice previews and confirmations, repeats, Back use, audio toggles, and practice milestones without storing message text.

## More precise missing-content reports

- The persistent action now says `Hier fehlt etwas`.
- It asks `Was fehlt hier?` and provides context-specific choices such as topic, answer, word, person, place, time, or help.
- Reports retain only the screen, path, visible option IDs, and missing-content type.
